# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS + Motion (Framer Motion), deployed on Vercel with server features available. Chosen by the user.

## Users

International travellers (families, groups of friends, wedding parties) planning a stay near Rome who want a private country house with a pool instead of a city hotel. They browse on a phone first, compare the houses and their capacity, then write to the owner to ask about availability and costs.

## Product Purpose

Casale del Gallo is a boutique country estate 15 km from the centre of Rome, at the foot of the Castelli Romani: three rentable houses (Casale San Carlo, Casale Sant'Antonio, Casale San Giorgio), private pools, a Mediterranean garden, a vineyard estate setting next to a golf course, plus services on request (private chef, transfers, concierge) and celebrations/weddings. The site exists to make a visitor want to stay and to send an enquiry about availability and costs. Success is an enquiry by WhatsApp or email.

## Positioning

Countryside retreat with Rome on the doorstep: 20 minutes by car from the Colosseum, 10 from Ciampino airport, inside a vineyard estate next to a golf course, with private pools (one black "cocktail pool") and views on the Castelli Romani.

## Operating Context

The owner, Giulio, answers enquiries personally by email (info@casaledelgallo.com) and WhatsApp (+39 349 260 1843). Address: Via di Fioranello, 00134 Roma, Italia. Instagram: casale_delgallo. No online booking, no published prices: "for any queries regarding availability and costs" the visitor writes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual: original English copy preserved verbatim, plus a faithful Italian translation, with an EN/IT switch.
- The contact form (Name, Email, Phone, Subject, Message) composes the message and opens it in WhatsApp or the email app; no backend.
- All original copy and all 80 original photos must be kept; no filler text.
- Pages: The Property (home), About, The Houses (+3 house pages), The Garden, Activities, Services, Wedding, Location, Contact.
- Original copy ends one sentence unfinished ("The golf club is closed on"); the day is unknown and must not be invented. User decision: omit the fragment until the closing day is supplied.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Casale del Gallo", tagline "Boutique home at the doors of Rome", the rooster weathervane mark (rooster over a compass arrow with S W O N). User brief: keep the Casale's identity and characteristic colours, raise it to elegant, warm, luxury, contemporary; mobile-first; 60fps motion using transform/opacity, no heavy backdrop-filter blur or rigid 3D.

## Evidence on Hand

Original photos (80) of the three houses' interiors, pools, garden, estate, golf course, aerial views, Rome landmarks, services; original copy for every page. No reviews, prices, ratings or awards exist on the source site: do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. The photographs sell the place; the interface frames them and gets out of the way.
2. Every fact on the page comes from the owner's own words.
3. Asking about availability is always one tap away.
4. Rome close, countryside around: show both sides of that promise.
