# Surface brief: Casale del Gallo website (all routes, EN/IT)

Scope: the whole site, home `/[lang]` plus the original pages (about, the-houses, three house pages, the-garden, activities, services, wedding, location, contactus). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: travellers planning a stay near Rome compare the three houses and ask Giulio about availability and costs. Action: WhatsApp or email enquiry, composed by the contact form. Proof: the owner's 80 original photographs and his own words. Constraints: all original copy kept (EN) plus faithful IT; no invented prices, reviews or sunrise times; transform/opacity motion only, no backdrop-filter, no 3D.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A day at the Casale, lit like a cyclorama. Scrolling moves the light across one seamless sky, from cobalt dawn over the Castelli Romani, through white noon and ochre afternoon, to terracotta sunset and cypress-black night. Refuses the villa template: drone hero with centred serif title over a row of equal room cards.

OWN-WORLD: fixed sky layers crossfading by section (iron night #0e110d, cobalt horizon #1f3a8a, Roman rose #e8a0a0, travertine day #efe7da, limewash ochre #d9a441, terracotta #b4532f, cypress #1f2a1c). A thin horizon rule glows at the colour change. Marcellus Roman capitals for headlines, Saira Stencil condensed caps and tabular numerals as lighting-cue labels carrying only real figures (15 km, 50 m, 10 x 5 x 1.5 m, 20 guests), Hanken Grotesk body. The rooster weathervane stands on the horizon as the only actor.

STORY: Rome is 20 minutes away but the day here belongs to the countryside; three houses, private pools, garden, golf next door, services on request; write to Giulio.

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-height dawn sky, horizon at 62%: the weathervane silhouette stands on it at right with a long shadow; "Casale del Gallo" in Marcellus caps spanning ~70% width left of it, tagline under it, real distance cues (15 km Roma, 5 km Ciampino) as stencil figures on the horizon; below the horizon the estate photo slideshow is the stage. Primary action "Ask availability" (WhatsApp) at the left under the tagline, email secondary; the day-dial at the bottom.

SIGNATURE: the day dial, a fixed arc where the sun travels as you scroll, naming the current phase (cue) and jumping to any phase on tap; the weathervane's shadow turns and shortens with the sun.

FORM: stagecraft cyclorama dawn (dealt challenger, chosen by the user), seed key ccdadc2d.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: the original golf text ends "The golf club is closed on" with no day; shown without the fragment until the owner supplies it.
