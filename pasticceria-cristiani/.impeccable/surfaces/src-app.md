# Surface brief: Pasticceria Cristiani one-page site

Scope: `/` (Italian), plus the single-file preview built from the same components. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: neighbours and Romans who want to see the cakes, pastries and salty trays and order by WhatsApp or phone, or come to Via dei Platani 123 D/E. Proof: the shop's own photographs, the five customer reviews the user confirmed as real, the opening hours given by the owner. Constraints: brief-pinned palette (panna, warm wood, discreet gold, dark contrasts), serif headings + sans text, scroll zoom into the counter, 60fps, no invented claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Walking into the shop. The scroll carries the visitor from the doorway to the counter: the shop photo zooms into the vitrine and dissolves into the pastries themselves; the vitrine's shelves then become the page. Refuses the bakery template of a centred title on a photo, a grid of equal product cards and a row of testimonial cards.

OWN-WORLD: panna #f5ede0 ground, mahogany #5b2418 from the counter, chocolate #241510 for the dark rooms, brass #b08d3c only as 1px rails and round fittings, glass highlight lines. Bodoni Moda (Italian Bodoni, shop-sign voice) for headings, Figtree for text, Petit Formal Script only on chocolate plaques, the hand-piped "Auguri" plaques of their cakes, used as category and reviewer labels.

STORY: a humble family pasticceria that became Centocelle's fixed point; everything shown comes from their counter; order a cake or a tray in one tap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the full-bleed counter photo; "Pasticceria Cristiani" in Bodoni at lower left over a chocolate falloff, "Centocelle · Roma" and the welcome line beneath, two actions: "Ordina su WhatsApp" (brass, primary) and "Vieni a trovarci" (ghost). An open-now plaque (live from the hours) at top right.

SIGNATURE: Ingresso al bancone: a pinned scroll zooms into the vitrine and crossfades into the TripAdvisor counter of pastries; then the four vitrine categories switch with a sliding brass pill (transitions.dev tabs) and the photos rise shelf by shelf.

FORM: brief-pinned direction (the user's own world: the shop, its counter and its chocolate plaques); concept roll not used because the brief pins palette, type and hero mechanic.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
