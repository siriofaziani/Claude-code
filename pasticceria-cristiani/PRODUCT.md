# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS + Motion, Italian only, one-page site; deploy target Vercel. A single-file preview (anteprima.html) is built from the same components for review in chat.

## Users

Families and neighbours of Centocelle (Rome) and people from across the city who order cakes for birthdays, baptisms, graduations and parties, buy mignon and pastries on Sunday, breakfast pastries in the morning, and salty trays (pizzette, tramezzini, rustici) for events. Mostly on a phone; they want to see the products and call or WhatsApp to order.

## Product Purpose

The website of Pasticceria Cristiani, Via dei Platani 123 D/E, 00172 Roma (Centocelle): a humble, historic, family-run pastry shop that has become a fixed point of quality and tradition in the neighbourhood. Success: an order or a visit (call, WhatsApp, directions).

## Positioning

The neighbourhood's own pasticceria: classic Roman pastry made by a family for decades, personalised celebration cakes with hand-piped chocolate plaques, and salty catering trays.

## Operating Context

Orders and custom cakes by phone (06 2323 2058) or WhatsApp (333 617 7105). Facebook page facebook.com/pasticc.cristiani.1958, Instagram @pasticceriacristianicentocelle, TripAdvisor listing. Opening hours from the owner: Monday closed; Tuesday to Saturday 7:30-13:00 and 15:30-20:00; Sunday 7:30-13:00 and 15:30-18:30.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: hero (counter photo, scroll zoom into the vitrines), Chi siamo, Le vetrine del gusto (4 categories), Recensioni, Ordina ora & contatti.
- No online shop or payments; ordering happens by WhatsApp/phone.
- Pending from the owner: holiday products photos (panettoni, colombe), confirmation of email (two different addresses online) and of the founding year. The TripAdvisor photo 2 of 11 (counter of pastry biscuits) is in use.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Pasticceria Cristiani" (Centocelle, Roma). Brief from the user: classy & gourmet, cream/panna tones, warm wood, discreet gold details and dark contrasts, no neon or odd colours; elegant serif headings with modern sans text; hero zoom into the counter on scroll; 60fps on phones.

Current brief (experimental full restyle, pinned by the user): "Boutique della Pasticceria Romana" in cream, gold and espresso; Awwwards / transitions.dev motion with Framer Motion for every transition and gesture, Aceternity UI / shadcn / Tailwind components; glass floating header with WhatsApp, Mappa, Chiama shortcuts; animated gold gradient/glow title; live open badge; product cards with 3D tilt and an animated category filter (Mignon, Torte su misura, Salato, Festività); an order wizard (Torte personalizzate, Mignon, Vassoio colazione, Festività) that sends a formatted WhatsApp message in one click; story with timeline/parallax; infinite moving review cards; info card with hours, embedded Google Map, click-to-call and WhatsApp. 60fps on phones, no layout shift, all real data unchanged. Glass and gradient text are explicitly requested here and override the usual no-glass/no-gradient-text rule.

## Evidence on Hand

- Counter/shop photo supplied in chat (hero).
- 24 photos from the owner's Google Drive (cakes, mignon, cannoli, bignè, maritozzo, tramezzini, pizzette, rustici).
- Five customer reviews supplied by the user, who confirmed they are real customer reviews (published as given, with links to TripAdvisor and Facebook).
- No awards, prices, founding date or statistics are confirmed: do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. The products are the hero; the interface is the shop window around them.
2. Ordering is one tap: call or WhatsApp from anywhere on the page.
3. Warm and humble, never flashy: tradition shown, not claimed.
4. Only facts the family or the user gave us.
